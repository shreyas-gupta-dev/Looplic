Common HGST 8 TB hard disk problems

Clicking or knocking sound

Usually points to damaged read/write heads or trouble reading the platters. Switch the drive off to avoid further damage.

Drive not detected

Can be caused by a PCB fault, a firmware issue, a power problem or an internal failure.

Firmware problem

The drive spins up but shows the wrong name or capacity, or your data can't be accessed.

Bad sectors or slow reading

Damaged sectors or weakening read heads can stop the drive from reading data reliably.

Accidental format or deleted files

Logical recovery is often possible if the data hasn't been overwritten, so stop using the drive straight away.

RAID or NAS failure

Data may need to be rebuilt from several drives while preserving the original RAID configuration.

MacBook data recovery

Your Mac won't start.
Your files still can.

Modern Macs store data on SSD chips soldered to the logic board and encrypted by Apple's security chip. You can't just pull the drive out — so our engineers repair the board itself, bring your original Mac back to life, and copy your data to a new drive.

How MacBook recovery works

  1. Free board-level diagnosisWe inspect the logic board under a microscope, find the failed components and confirm what's recoverable.
  2. Repair to extractCorroded or shorted parts are replaced so your original Mac can power on — the only way to read a soldered, encrypted SSD.
  3. Secure copyWith your login password, your files are copied to a new encrypted drive and verified before you pay.

Before you bring your Mac in

  • Don't keep switching on a liquid-damaged Mac — each attempt can short the board.
  • Don't reinstall macOS or erase the disk in Recovery mode.
  • Don't run Disk Utility First Aid again and again on a failing drive.
  • Note your Mac login / FileVault password — it's needed to unlock the data.
  • Tell us your model (Apple menu → About This Mac, or the serial on the base).

Can you recover data from a MacBook that won't turn on?

Often, yes. On T2 and Apple silicon (M1–M4) Macs the SSD is soldered to the logic board and hardware-encrypted, so the storage can't simply be removed and read elsewhere. Switch it off, unplug the charger, and don't try to power it on again. Each attempt can spread corrosion or short components. Contact us on WhatsApp or call +91 98458 63602 — the sooner the board is cleaned, the better the chance of recovery.
